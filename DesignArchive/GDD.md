
## Basic INFO :-

**Genre:** Mystery Horror.
**Camera**: AUTO Multiple Camera (Top-down , Side).
**Art Style**: Little Nightmares inspired ,Stylized ,Dark ,Low-mid poly.

**GamePLAY LOOP**: Investigate --> Puzzles  ---> Unlock area ---> Boss fight;

**Gameplay**: Investigate ,Solve puzzles ,Boss fight.
**Length**: *20-25 minutes*.
**Controls**: *WASD, Interact, Run, Flashlight*.
**Platform**: PC/WEB/Smart-Phone
**Theme**: Detective , horror, *One boss*.

# Core Pillars

- Exploration should reward curiosity.
- Horror comes from atmosphere, not jump scares.
- Every puzzle reveals more of the story.
- Boss is the climax, not the main gameplay.

# STORY

## Prologue

A famous scientist is found dead inside a secret research facility.

The police send you, a detective, because there are **no signs of forced entry** and every security camera stopped recording at the exact moment of the murder.

The building has been sealed.

You are the first person allowed inside.

---

## Investigation

As you explore, you discover:

- The victim wasn't just a scientist.
- The laboratory was secretly researching an unknown organism.
- Every room reveals another piece of the experiment.
- Staff notes become more desperate.
- Some pages are torn.
- Blood stains become more frequent.
- The deeper you go, the stranger the facility becomes.

Eventually you discover that

**The victim wasn't killed by a person.**

He was killed by **Subject-01**.

---

## Twist

The elevator needs authorization from the Office.

After restoring power and unlocking security,

you activate the Secret Elevator.

Instead of escaping...

it takes you **below the laboratory.**

There is one final file.

> "If you're reading this... we're already dead. Do NOT open Chamber 01."

Too late.

The chamber begins opening.

Alarm.

Red lights.

Power failure.

The Subject-01 escapes.

---

## Boss Fight

Subject-01 is the only survivor of the experiments.

It's unstable.

Fast.

Huge.

The detective uses clues collected throughout the investigation to understand its weakness.

Fight.

Escape.

Ending.

## Special things about story

✅ Very few cutscenes.

✅ Story is told through the environment.

✅ Fits the horror atmosphere.

✅ Naturally leads to a boss.

✅ Easy to build in 15 days.
